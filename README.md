# Raza Photos

Browse your Mac photo library on any device at home — no cloud, no subscriptions, no setup.

## What it does

Raza Photos turns your Mac into a private photo server. Open the app on your Mac and build your library, then browse it instantly from your iPhone or iPad over your home Wi-Fi. Your photos never leave your local network. 

## Features

- [Import your local photo library](https://raduzaharia-medium.github.io/raza-photos-support/METADATA_READ)
- [Write metadata](https://raduzaharia-medium.github.io/raza-photos-support/METADATA_WRITE) back to the photos in the library
- Seamless network discovery and connectivity
- Multiple servers with separate libraries, all devices will see all photos
- Tag photos with albums, people, events and locations on any device in the network
- Browse photos in grid and map view
- Offline face detection and grouping
- Application is fully offline, with no telemetry

## Requirements

- Mac running macOS 26 or later
- iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later
- All devices on the same Wi-Fi network

## Privacy Policy

Raza Photos collects no personal data. No telemetry, no analytics, no tracking. Your photos and metadata never leave your local network. Nothing is transmitted to any server outside your home.
